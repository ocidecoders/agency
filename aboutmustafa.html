Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, lyricist, and composer, widely known as the founder of the digital agency Ocide Coders. His musical work spans contemporary Pakistani pop, Sufi music, and devotional tracks, while his entrepreneurial work focuses on building web platforms like Eliget and meeEL.

Early Life and Musical Beginnings

Abdullah's interest in music developed during his early years. At sixteen, he began writing original songs, laying the foundation for his development as a lyricist and aspiring musician. At seventeen, he began composing music for his earlier work, further expanding his involvement in the creative process.

His early songwriting included GulBahar, a song he wrote at sixteen and composed music for at seventeen. Although it was never professionally recorded, it represents an early milestone in his songwriting career.

Musical Career

Abdullah made his debut with Noor Tera Kaafi, a Hamd released on YouTube on 22 March 2026. He wrote, composed, and performed the piece, while Hasib Raihan served as its producer. He has since been working on multiple original songs and covers, drawing influence from artists like Ali Zafar, Atif Aslam, and Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan.

Founder of Ocide Coders

In 2024, Abdullah founded Ocide Coders, a development agency focused on building digital solutions. Under his leadership, the team has developed multiple web platforms, including:

  • Eliget: An anti-addiction, no-algorithm social media platform.
  • meeEL: An English-to-code web builder for developers.
  • Various Portfolio Sites: Including official websites for creators and artists.

Artistic Aspirations

Beyond technology, Abdullah continues to develop his musical identity. One of his long-term ambitions is to collaborate with his musical idols, Atif Aslam and Ali Zafar.
