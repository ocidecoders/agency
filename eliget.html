Social media today is broken. It is designed to keep you scrolling, to show you videos you didn't ask for, and to feed you content based on secret algorithms. Eliget is the antidote.

Built by Ocide Coders, Eliget is a social network for people who think. It is a space where you can share your thoughts without being manipulated by an algorithm.

No Algorithms. No Videos.

We removed everything that makes social media toxic:

  • No Algorithm: You see posts chronologically. No secret AI decides what you should think.
  • Text-Only: No short-form videos, no endless reels. Just pure, written thoughts.
  • No Infinite Scroll: You read what your friends shared, and then you log off and live your life.

The Key to a Free Website

We believe in building a community. If you join Eliget and follow @ocidecoders, you become eligible to request a completely free single-page portfolio website built by our team.

It's our way of giving back to creators who value their time and attention.
