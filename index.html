FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter is a single-page website — perfect if you want to test us. Business includes multiple pages, custom design, SEO setup, and a contact form.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. You tell us your idea, we handle every technical detail. You receive a fully-working website ready to launch.

Can you really build a website for $1?

Yes. The Starter package is genuinely $1 — one page, three sections, mobile responsive, delivered in 24–48 hours. It's our way of letting you test our quality before committing to something bigger.

Will my website appear on Google?

Every site we build comes with a proper SEO foundation — meta tags, sitemap, schema markup, and a Google Search Console setup. You'll be indexed within days.

Who owns the code once it's built?

You do. We hand over the full repository on delivery — no lock-in, no ongoing fees unless you want our support.

How do I pay?

For Starter projects, we handle it simply. For Business and Custom work, we'll agree on a payment method (PayPal, crypto, or bank transfer) before starting.
