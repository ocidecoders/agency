Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
What is Ocide Coders?

Ocide Coders is a premium web development agency founded by Abdullah Al Mustafa in 2024. We build fast, modern, and SEO-optimized websites for creators and businesses, and we also develop our own digital products like Eliget and meeEL.

How can I get a completely free website?

It's simple! We believe in building a community. To get a free single-page portfolio, you just need to join our social platform Eliget, follow the @ocidecoders page, and send us a message requesting a site. Our team will build it for you.

How much do your paid websites cost?

Our pricing is designed to be affordable. The Starter plan begins at just $6 for a single-page site with full code access. The Standard plan is $12 (includes 1 week of edits), and the Premium plan is $44, which includes a custom .me domain, backend setup, and Supabase integration.

Do I own the website after it is built?

Yes, absolutely. Unlike website builders that lock you into their platform, we provide you with the full source code (HTML/CSS/JS) and give you access to the hosting accounts (Render, Netlify, GitHub). You have 100% ownership.

What is the difference between Eliget and meeEL?

Both are products created by Ocide Coders. Eliget is an anti-addiction, text-only social network where you can share your thoughts without algorithms. meeEL is a natural language web builder that allows non-coders to build web pages by giving simple English commands.

Can you help me get a domain name?

Yes. If you choose our Premium plan, we will register a custom .me domain for you (like yourname.me). If you choose a cheaper plan, you can still buy a domain from providers like Namecheap or Porkbun, and we will connect it to your website.
